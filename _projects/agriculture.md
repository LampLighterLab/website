Feeding a growing population sustainably is one of the [grand challenges of our generation](https://www.un.org/en/global-issues/food).
We explore how the AI and robotics tools developed through our research can be applied to agriculture.
While this thrust offers ample opportunities to explore both tools and connections to biology, the focus is on practical, real-world outcomes.

Current projects include:

**Using robotics RL tools to understand how to help horses recover from anesthesia**

**Early detection of lameness and gait anomalies in livestock, using embodied latent-dynamics models**
