## Work together

We're always open to collaborations, and we believe the best collaborations happen organically. If you're interested in exchanging ideas, let's start with a conversation, without commitment, and see what happens.

Our interests span [animal movement, robotics and learning, and agriculture]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}). A shared question can be the beginning of something useful.

## Graduate students

Graduate study begins with admission to a Cornell graduate program, most likely in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAE).

If you already have a primary advisor or external funding and are interested in working together, we're open to discussing shared research interests.
