## Student applications

Please reach out to Steve Heim (heim.steve-at-cornell.edu) with a brief description of your interests and why you'd like to work with us. Include your CV and transcript.
Generally, you will need to be enrolled at Cornell. Prospective graduate students are welcome to reach out and discuss their interests with faculty, but admission to Cornell is a separate process.

Please read our [Expectations and Culture]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}#expectations-and-culture) section before reaching out.
You can also find an incomplete list of open projects on [Cornell's undergraduate and M.Eng. projects site](https://ug-meng-projects.coecis.cornell.edu) (Cornell sign-in required).
